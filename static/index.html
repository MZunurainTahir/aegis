💡 Understanding Aegis & "Run AI Cycle"

🎯 What does "Run AI Cycle" do?

The core requirement of the Alpaca Hackathon is an Autonomous AI Trading Agent. When you click Run AI Cycle (or enable the 24/7 background loop), Aegis executes a sequential triad:

  1. Quant Agent: Pulls live options chains across SPY, QQQ, AAPL, NVDA, TSLA, and calculates Black-Scholes Greeks (Δ, Θ, V) and annualized yields.
  2. Sentiment Agent: Queries multi-LLM consensus (Groq Llama 3.3, Gemini Flash, DeepSeek) to evaluate news catalysts and earnings dates.
  3. Risk Governance Agent: Evaluates collateral against strict position size limits. If approved, it automatically routes the trade to Alpaca Paper Trading.

⚡ How to Deploy Individual Trades Manually?

In the Quant Screened Wheel Opportunities table, click the green "Deploy" button next to any candidate contract to submit the trade ticket directly to your Alpaca paper brokerage account.
