Understanding Aegis ALPHA & "Run AI Cycle"

What does "Run AI Cycle" do?

The core requirement of the Alpaca Hackathon is an Autonomous AI Trading Agent. When you click Run AI Cycle (or enable the 24/7 background loop), Aegis executes a full institutional pipeline:

  1. Portfolio Manager: Sweeps ALL open positions first — takes profit at 50%, stops losses at 200%, rolls ITM contracts, and sweeps expiries. Realized P&L is journaled instantly.
  2. News Agent: Ingests LIVE headlines via Google News RSS and classifies the market regime (RISK_ON / NEUTRAL / RISK_OFF / CRISIS).
  3. Quant Agent: Pulls live options chains across SPY, QQQ, AAPL, NVDA, TSLA + more, computes Black-Scholes Greeks, IV rank, and annualized yields — with duplicate-position guards.
  4. Sentiment Agent: Multi-LLM consensus (Groq, Cerebras, Gemini, DeepSeek) scores conviction 1-100 on REAL news. Earnings blackout enforced.
  5. Risk Governance Agent: Evaluates collateral against strict position limits. If approved, the trade routes to Alpaca Paper Trading automatically.
  6. Tail Hedge Engine: Independently monitors a live SPY-IV stress index and buys protective puts when volatility spikes.

How to Deploy Individual Trades Manually?

In the Quant Screened Wheel Opportunities table, click the green "Deploy" button next to any candidate contract to submit the trade ticket directly to your Alpaca paper brokerage account.

Where do performance numbers come from?

All analytics (equity curve, win rate, Sharpe, drawdown) are computed from the SQLite trade journal — every order, close, roll and expiry is persisted and attributed. No hardcoded backtest numbers.
